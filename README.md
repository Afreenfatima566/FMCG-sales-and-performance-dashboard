# FMCG-sales-and-performance-dashboard
🌶️ FMCG Spices and Pickles Sales Performance Dashboard
12-Month FMCG Sales & Profitability Overview
1. Project Title / Headline

Spices and Pickles Sales Performance Dashboard
A comprehensive Power BI dashboard providing a 12-month overview of sales, profitability, and distributor performance in the FMCG spices and pickles segment.

2. Short Description / Purpose

This dashboard is designed to analyze and monitor the sales performance, profit trends, and product profitability of a spices and pickles FMCG business. It enables stakeholders to quickly assess overall business health, identify top-performing products and distributors, and support data-driven decision-making.

3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

📊 Power BI Desktop – Primary platform for building interactive data visualizations
🔄 Power Query – Used for data cleaning, transformation, and preparation
🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Created measures such as total revenue, total profit, profit margin %, and ranking logic
🧩 Data Modeling – Relationships established across sales, product, category, and distributor fields
📁 File Format – .pbix for development and .png for dashboard preview

4. Data Source

Source: Simulated FMCG sales dataset (Spices & Pickles domain)

Data includes:
Monthly sales data across a 12-month period
Product categories (Spices, Pickles, Blended & Whole Spices)
Distributor-wise revenue contribution
Profit and profit margin percentages
Units sold and pricing attributes
The dataset is structured to reflect a real-world FMCG distribution and sales scenario.

5. Features / Highlights
🔹 Business Problem

FMCG businesses often struggle to quickly answer questions such as:
Which products and distributors drive the most revenue?
How do revenue and profit trends change month over month?
Which products generate high margins and should be prioritized?
How balanced is the sales contribution across distributors?
Raw sales data alone does not provide these insights clearly or efficiently.

🔹 Goal of the Dashboard

The goal of this dashboard is to:
Provide an executive-level summary of sales and profitability
Enable trend analysis across months
Identify top products and distributors
Highlight high-margin products for strategic focus
Support inventory planning and distribution decisions

🔹 Walkthrough of Key Visuals
📌 Key KPIs (Top Section)

Total Revenue – Overall sales generated in the selected period
Total Profit – Net profit contribution
Profit Margin % – Overall business profitability
Top Distributor – Distributor with the highest revenue contribution
Top Product – Best-selling product by revenue
These KPIs provide a quick health check of the business.

📈 Monthly Revenue & Profit Trend (Combo Chart)

Displays monthly revenue (bars) and profit (line)
Helps identify seasonality, growth patterns, and performance dips
Enables comparison of distributor-level trends

🍩 Sales Contribution by Distributor (Donut Chart)

Shows the percentage contribution of each distributor
Helps evaluate dependency and performance balance across distributors

📊 High Margin Products (>25%) (Bar Chart)

Highlights products with profit margins above 25%
Supports pricing strategy, promotion, and premium positioning
Helps management focus on the most profitable items

🎛️ Interactive Slicers

Month
Category
Distributor
These slicers allow users to dynamically filter all visuals and explore specific business scenarios.

🔹 Business Impact & Insights

Profit Optimization: High-margin products can be prioritized for promotions and wider distribution
Distributor Strategy: Clear visibility into distributor contribution helps manage risk and performance
Seasonal Awareness: Monthly trends support demand forecasting and inventory planning
Executive Readiness: Clean, uncluttered layout ensures fast decision-making
